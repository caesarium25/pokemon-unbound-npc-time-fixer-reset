Pokémon Unbound NPC Time Fixer Reset

Re-enables the one-time Frozen Heights Time Fixer NPC for a specific RTC-tampering recovery scenario. Processing is entirely local in your browser.

Before using: keep an untouched backup and make sure your emulator/device date and time are correct.

Status 
Choose a .sav file.

After patching

  1. Put the patched .sav into your emulator.
  2. Launch Unbound. The RTC warning may still appear on this first boot.
  3. Load your save.
  4. Talk to the Time Fixer NPC in Frozen Heights, outside Professor Log's Lab.
  5. Let the NPC repair the RTC.
  6. Save normally in-game.
  7. Restart the game. The RTC warning should be gone.

This is an unofficial community repair. It does not include or distribute Pokémon ROMs or save data.
